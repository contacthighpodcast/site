What is this, exactly

CHP 101

Contact High is a comedy podcast. Riley and Michael get in a room, sometimes after 2am, and somehow an hour or two later they've ranked religions by how much they poop or simultaneously come to the conclusion that Asian Santa Claus has a Fu Manchu, not a beard. No plans. No scripts. No guardrails. No corporate overlords. A driving test story turns into He-Man discourse turns into a caveman getting chemo, and by the time anyone notices, a new episode is ready for your viewing pleasure.

We've talked to a French comedian who got sued for a joke, argued about which politicians are actually dead, read Gandhi's actual letters to Hitler out loud, and gone off on more unhinged rants that nobody should ever miss out on.

Full video episodes drop every Monday on YouTube and Spotify, with audio everywhere else. Come for the guests, stay because you have no idea what's happening and neither do we.

Edited by Sam Sawyer, who has somehow made all of this watchable.
