What is this, exactly

the pitch

Contact High is a weekly comedy podcast that starts with one topic and ends somewhere completely different. Think caveman chemotherapy, Asian Santa Claus, and whether Batman would vote Republican — all in the same episode.

Hosted by Riley Flanagan and Michael Fleming, edited by Sam Sawyer.

New full episodes drop weekly on YouTube and Spotify, with clips scattered across everywhere else.
